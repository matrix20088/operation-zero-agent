## Request a quote

1. [Open a new GitHub issue](https://github.com/matrix20088/operation-zero-agent/issues/new) titled **Paid task: [one sentence]**. Include the desired result, sample *non-confidential* input and output, budget, deadline, and objective acceptance tests.
2. Do **not** put passwords, account information, personal records, or confidential company files in a public issue.
3. We will clarify scope and agree on an escrow-backed payment method, if available, before starting. An issue or conversation is **not** a paid order.
4. The final deliverable includes files, a change log, and test evidence. Requests involving fake accounts, fake reviews, credential extraction, financial transfers, or unlawful work are not accepted.

**Fulfillment:** Human-reviewed AI-assisted deliverables. No deceptive claim of human-only authorship. Availability and deadline are agreed per contract. No statement on this page implies a previous client, sale, or payout.
